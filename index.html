About Me

By day, I am a software architect who genuinely enjoys crafting and exploring - not just building systems, but thinking deeply about cloud architecture, CI/CD, DevSecOps, and the security mindset that ties everything together.

Outside work, running is my way to escape screens, clear my head, and let ideas reorganize themselves. I take part to official running and trail events, maybe to push my limits, maybe to collect goodies and medals.

Europe feels more like my home than any single border, and traveling across Europe feeds my identity.
